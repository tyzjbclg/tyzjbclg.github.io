Phase-Adaptive Fusion: Spatio-Temporal Modulation for VLA Models

Junhao Zhu · Second author

Phase-Adaptive Fusion (PAF) is a lightweight plug-in for Vision-Language-Action models that addresses over-reliance on proprioceptive states. It combines language-guided spatial alignment with temporal phase estimation to dynamically strengthen visual features during alignment-critical stages, improving spatial grounding and robustness in robotic manipulation.
